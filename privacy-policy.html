Privacy Policy — Cappy TikTok Poster

Last updated: 2026-09-12

This app connects to the TikTok API on behalf of a single TikTok account (the developer's own) to automate posting affiliate marketing videos. It is not a public consumer product — there are no third-party end users, no sign-up flow, and no accounts other than the one it's authorized against.

What this app touches

  • TikTok authorization tokens (OAuth access token and refresh token) for the one TikTok account it's connected to, used solely to call TikTok's Content Posting API on that account's behalf.
  • Video files generated locally for posting — short marketing videos produced by the developer's own video-generation pipeline, uploaded to TikTok only for the purpose of publishing or drafting them as posts.

What this app does not touch

  • No personal data belonging to anyone other than the account holder.
  • No data from TikTok viewers, followers, or any other TikTok user.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising identifiers of any kind — this is a backend automation tool, not a website or consumer-facing app.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. The only external service this app communicates with is TikTok's own API, which is the platform the videos are being posted to.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored privately on infrastructure controlled solely by the developer, never logged in plaintext, and never shared with any other system, service, or automated agent beyond this integration itself.
  • Video files exist locally only until they've been uploaded to TikTok; this app does not retain a separate copy or archive of posted content beyond what TikTok itself stores as the published post.

Revoking access

The account holder can revoke this app's access at any time from TikTok's own app-permissions settings, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.
